Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out in the hollows.

What stays on your phone

West Virginia Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is West Virginia Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

Two kinds of source. The pepperoni roll, hot dog, Italian, ramp, Appalachian table and oldest-places lists are our own research from fall 2026, with a dated 2025 or 2026 source or West Virginia Tourism's own listing behind each place. The wider directory of 3,339 restaurants starts from open map listings (Overture Maps), filtered to the kinds that proved reliable where states publish restaurant lists.

Where can I get a pepperoni roll?

Our pepperoni roll list has 11 hand-checked stops on the state's Pepperoni Roll Trail, from Country Club Bakery in Fairmont, which the state calls the home of the original, to the Clarksburg bakeries. In the web search, the list puts the nearest first once you share your location.

How do I check a West Virginia restaurant's inspection?

West Virginia's local health departments inspect restaurants; none posts a letter grade or publishes results as data, and many post reports only on a search portal. So the app shows no inspection results: each place links to the state's food safety page, and we compute no grade.

Does it show which places serve alcohol?

No. West Virginia's Alcohol Beverage Control Administration licenses the bars and restaurants that pour alcohol, but its license search posts no terms of use, so the app doesn't say either way.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
